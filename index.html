Stage 01: Before You Begin

Set the trapline.
Catch what shouldn't get through.

Catch it before it catches you.

Trapline is a short phishing-awareness exercise built around the messages non-interest (Islamic) finance staff actually see: murabaha financing notices, ijarah leasing prompts, musharaka partnership requests, and sukuk payout alerts. It takes about 12 to 15 minutes.

Before you start

  • 01Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point without any consequence.
  • 02You'll be identified only by an anonymous code, never your name, staff ID, or email.
  • 03You'll answer 10 scenario questions twice: once before and once after a short interactive walkthrough.
  • 04Your responses are stored securely and reported only in aggregate, alongside other participants.
  • 05There are no trick questions designed to embarrass you. This is a learning exercise, not a test of character.
